COLORSHINE MES V2 v0.10.4 - MASTER CREATION

1. Stop backend and frontend.
2. Extract/copy patch over:
   D:\Colorshine\Color_MES\ColorshineMes
3. Keep your existing backend/frontend .env files.
4. Run:
   backend\APPLY_V0104_MASTER_CREATION.bat
5. Confirm console shows bad_tolerance_rows = 0.
6. Restart backend and frontend.
7. Browser: Ctrl + F5.

IMPORTANT:
Finished/GL and CR thickness Min/Max are Target +/- 0.005 mm.
0.050 mm is NOT used.

No Year/Month/Day capacity is seeded because the workbook did not provide capacity values.
